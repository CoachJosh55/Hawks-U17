Our System

How We Play

Offense

Share It

Our offense is built on continuous movement. We break teams down through rapid ball movement and spacing, not individual isolations.

  • Drive and Kick Collapse the paint to force help, then find the open shooter.
  • Corner Spacing Gravity matters. Stay wide to create driving lanes and pull defenders out.
  • Good to Great Turn down a "good" contested shot to make one extra pass for a "great" open look.
  • Tactical Sets We use tools like the Hammer Series and Spain P&R to force defensive mistakes.
Defense

No Middle

We dictate where the opponent goes. Defense is an active choice, utilizing the sidelines and baseline as our extra defenders.

  • Force Baseline Never allow the ball handler access to the middle of the paint. Dictate their path.
  • The Low Man Helps Rim protection is a team responsibility, led by the furthest weak-side defender.
  • X-Out Rotations When help comes, we scramble and rotate back out as a single connected five-man unit.
  • Talk or Sit If you are quiet, you are a liability. Calling screens and assignments is mandatory.
The Hawks Culture

The Non-Negotiables

🚪

Leave Ego at the Door

It's about the name on the front of the jersey, not the back. Team success is our only metric.

⚡

Next Play Mentality

Mistakes happen. Don't dwell on them. Your job is to make the very next play count.

💼

No-Nonsense Attitude

We show up on time, ready to work. We don't waste our teammates' time with distractions.

🔥

Effort is Your Job

The coach shouldn't have to motivate you to play hard. Effort is the price of entry.

🤝

Pick Each Other Up

Celebrate your teammates. If someone falls or makes a mistake, be the first one there to lift them.

📉

Process over Results

We focus on getting better every day. If we execute the system, the wins will take care of themselves.
