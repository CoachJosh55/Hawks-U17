U17 Introductory Playbook

The Hammer Action

Our identity. We move the ball to the Pinch Post to create a high-speed downhill drive. This forces the defense to make a choice under pressure.

The 3 Primary Reads

1
The Finish If the lane is open, we attack the rim for a layup. Finish with strength.
2
The Corner Kick If the weak-side defender helps on your drive, kick the ball to the corner for the open 3.
3
The Dump to Roller If the defense stays home on the shooter, the screener rolling/ diving to the hoop will be wide open.
SLOB Mastery: This is exactly what we run for Sideline Out-of-Bounds. Same spots, same reads.

No-Middle Defense

We protect the house. We have one core rule that never changes: Take away the middle.

1. Force Baseline Position your feet to funnel the ball-handler toward the sideline. Never allow a middle drive.
2. Pre-Load Help If you are away from the ball, you must be in the lane before the drive happens.
3. High Communication Call "Help" and "Baseline" on every possession. We defend as five, not one.
