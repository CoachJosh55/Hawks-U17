Movement Prep

U17 Dynamic Stretching Protocol

Phase 01

Neck Mobility

Exercise 01
Neck Roll (Half-Circle)
Neck FlexorsTraps

Walk normally, drop chin to chest then slowly roll ear to shoulder on each side. No full circles.

Loosens the cervical spine and prepares for scanning the court.

Phase 02

Upper Body & Thoracic

Exercise 02
Arm Circle + Cross Swing
DeltoidsRotator Cuff

Walk forward swinging both arms in large, controlled circles, then swing arms across the chest like a hug.

Warms the glenohumeral joint for shooting and passing actions.

Exercise 03
Overhead Reach + Lean
LatsObliques

Interlace fingers, press palms to ceiling and lean gently to one side with each step.

Elongates the lats and improves reach for rebounds.

Exercise 04
Walking Thoracic Rotation
T-SpinePecs

Hands behind head, elbows wide. With each step, rotate your upper chest fully toward the lead leg.

Crucial for rotational power and maintaining vision while moving.

Phase 03

Core & Hip Stability

Exercise 05
Walking Lunge + Rotation
Hip FlexorsGlutes

Step into a lunge, lower the back knee, then rotate both arms and chest over the front knee.

Prepares the hips for low defensive stances and explosive drives.

Exercise 06
High Knees Pull
GlutesCore

Pull one knee into the chest with both hands while rising onto the opposite tiptoe.

Opens the hip flexor while loading the glute for vertical power.

Exercise 07
Lateral Lunge Walk
AdductorsGroin

Step wide to the side, sit into the hip while keeping the trailing leg straight. Alternate sides.

Essential for lateral defensive sliding and "No Middle" containment.

Exercise 08
Cossack Walk
Deep Hip Mobility

Similar to lateral lunge but lower. Shift weight from side to side while staying as low as possible.

Extreme range of motion prep for awkward landing recovery.

Phase 04

Posterior Chain & Ankles

Exercise 09
Leg Kick Superman
HamstringsGlutesLower Back

Walk forward and kick one straight leg up toward the outstretched opposite hand, then immediately swing that same leg back behind you, hinging forward into a "flying superman" balance with arms reaching forward for 1-2 seconds.

Combines an active hamstring kick with a posterior-chain swing-through to challenge single-leg balance and control.

Exercise 10
Inchworm Walk-out
HamstringsShoulders

Hinge and walk hands out to a plank position, hold 1 second, then walk feet back to hands.

Comprehensive posterior chain activation and core bracing.

Exercise 11
Heel-to-Glute Pull
QuadsKnee Extensors

Pull one heel toward your glute while rising onto the opposite tiptoe. Hold 1 second.

Actively stretches the rectus femoris to prevent quad strains.

Exercise 12
Knee-Over-Toe Lunge
VMOPatella Tendon

Step into a lunge and consciously push the front knee forward past the toes while keeping heel down.

Builds knee resilience and reduces injury risk during deceleration.

Exercise 13
Walking Calf Raise
AchillesCalves

Walk forward only on tiptoes, driving up as high as possible on each step.

Warms the Achilles and prepares for explosive jumping.

Exercise 14
Ankle Roll Walk
Tibialis AnteriorAnkle Stability

Walk on your heels with toes lifted as high as possible for the full court width.

Trains the tibialis anterior to absorb impact during landing.
