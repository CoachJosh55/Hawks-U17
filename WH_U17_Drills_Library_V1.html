3 Man Lays
Finishing Passing
▶
Split into teams of 5. 3 cones (blocks and basket). 30 seconds to make the highest score. Place 2 men in jail spaced 45° back from the blocks — unlock with a physical touch (high-five or spud) who then runs onto the pass. No dribbles. Coach with a blocking pad to contest layups.
Pattern
Basket → Pass → Unlock → Cut → Layup → Unlock → Rebound → Repeat
Format
Teams of 5
Timer
30 sec
Scoring
Paint make = 3 pts · Shy-away make = 1 pt · Every coach block = 5 pts. Team scores.

60 Layups
Finishing Defence
▶
Offensive team and defensive team — stay on same team. Offense at NBA wing each with ball. Defence at opposite block. Coach at elbow passes for a cut and finish. New O and D player each rep. Either catch off run and finish, or 1 dribble allowed. Play off 2 feet. Defence must rotate and wall up.
Timer
1 min
Goal
60 pts
Scoring
Paint make = 5 pts · Outside paint = 1 pt · Defence stop = 6 pts · Block = 10 pts. Team scores.

2v1 off 2 Feet
Finishing Defence
▶
3 lines under basket, defender in middle. Pass to coach at FTL and coach decides who to pass to for a 2-foot power finish. Whole group out, 1 shot only, 1 pass only. Defender must wall up in cylinder to avoid fouling. Progress to elbow catch for a rip-thru drive.
Scoring
Paint make = 5 pts · Outside paint = 1 pt · Block ends the drill. Whole team score.

Cone Work
Finishing Ball Handling
▶
Must use traffic cones so players can pick up. Players start on wing, corner or top with ball. Coach plays static defence in a stance (shade 1 side) with a traffic cone behind him. Player executes a dribble move (BTB, BTL or push cross) and blows by. Player MUST pick up traffic cone with weak hand and get to basket for a finish. Cone pickup forces shoulder dip — essential for blowing by defenders and avoiding a wide driving angle. 2nd coach under basket with blocking pad.
Scoring
No cone pickup = no score · Paint make = 5 pts · Smile make = 10 pts · Target: 100 pts.

Full Court Layup Race
Finishing Defence
▶
4 teams (O/D) on both baselines, queue at each paint line. 2 players racing to other basket for a layup or stop. Offensive player has 2 balls — dribble both to halfcourt, then 1 ball in frontcourt (hold 2nd under arm). Defensive player can go once they pass FTL. Can finish with either hand on either side. Both sides going at the same time.
Scoring
Steal = 5 pts · Made layup = 10 pts · Target: 50 pts.

Finishing Defence

Finishing Defence

Square up to Player B with hips and chest, 1 hard dribble and blow by. Either get to a paint finish or a pull up. Swap every 3 possessions.

Corner 2 Corner
Shooting
▶
Split into trios. 1 minute — 2 rebounders with 2 balls. Player sprints along baseline from corner to corner looking to catch and shoot in deep corner. Rotate shooter after 1 minute. Highest score wins.
Scoring
3pt make = 10 pts · Target: 100 pts — if achieved drill ends.

Corner Chase
Shooting Defence
▶
3 equal teams: Shooters, Screeners, Defenders. Shooters start at NBA wing. Screeners start in no-man's land. Defender starts in gap between coach (top) and shooter. Pass from wing to top, screener sets a legal screen shading towards corner. Cut off screen to corner for contested 3. Start with shooting only, then add pump into sidestep. If open, let the ball fly.
Scoring
3pt make = 5 pts · Pump and sidestep = 3 pts · Stop = 5 pts · Block ends the drill · Target: 25 pts. O/D team scores.

Grinch
Shooting System
▶
5-spot shooting — system shots only: Layup, WS corner 3, SS corner 3, Elbow 2, Wing 3. Split into teams of 5, 3 from another team act as baseline extra passers. Teams of 5 go on and off together in Green Eggs spacing. PG passes to Pinch Post and cuts for layup → WS corner 3 → SS corner 3 → Elbow 2 → WS Wing 3. Go until goal is achieved or 10 mins.
Team Goals per trip
4 makes = at least once · 3 makes = at least twice · 2 makes = at least thrice · 1 make = at least four times. Higher goals stack down if repeated.

Mini Grinch
Shooting System
▶
Split into trios: elbow player, wing player, opposite corner player. 2 defenders — 1 on the nail, 1 under basket. 1 static defender calling from behind baseline. Wing player sprints to collect ball from coach at halfcourt. Defender picks up whoever they like. Wing initiates through the wing with a skip pass or high-post entry. Looking for a cut and score. 3 possessions to set a score, then swap — 2nd team wins if they beat it.
Scoring
Corner 3 = 30 pts · Any other 3 = 10 pts · Any layup = 20 pts · Midrange = 1 pt.

Shooting
▶
2 players, 1 ball each. 6 cones lined up behind with a tennis ball in the middle. Each make (elbow jumpers) moves the tennis ball to the closer cone. First to push the ball to their end cone wins. Can also play tic-tac-toe, or split into equal teams. Rotate people on each hoop.

2v1 Shooting
Shooting Defence
▶
Offense and defence groups — stay on until target score is met. 2 shooters at corner and wing (Green Eggs spacing). Dedicated passer (3 possessions) at opposite elbow starts with ball. Defender in cover 2 help — 1 foot on paint line. Elbow passes to either shooter, defender closes out. Shooters allowed 1 pass. No pump fakes to start.
Scoring
Wing 3 = 10 pts · Corner 3 = 20 pts · Steals and blocks = 15 pts · Target: 50 pts. Team scoring.

3v2 Shooting
Shooting Defence
▶
O and D teams. 3 offensive players spaced around 3pt line. 2 defenders start at elbows. 2 balls start with defenders and are passed out to offensive players. No dribbling allowed. Offensive players can swap spots. 2 defenders must close out, recover, rebound, and pass back. 45 seconds to score as many as possible. After all players have played, swap O/D.
Scoring
3pt make = 5 pts · Stop = 5 pts · Steal = 10 pts. O/D team scoring.

Rip Thru into Reverse Pivot Pass
Passing Ball Handling
▶
Pairs line up opposite each other across the whole court. Player A rips thru and drives towards Player B — the rip thru is the trigger for Player B to relocate to Player A's original position. Player A jumpstops and reverse pivots. Pass series: one-handed, over the top, top fake bounce pass.

Seated Passing Race
Passing Warmup
▶
Split group into 2 teams sat in single file line facing each other (in pairs, touching shoes) along paint lines. 1 ball per team. 5 passes between each pair before pass over the top to next pair and relocate to front of sitting line. Race to other baseline, piggy back race back. Touch passes are important, counting out loud is important — if ball touches floor start that pair's count again. Use medicine ball to make harder.

4v3 Around Key
Passing Defence
▶
O and D teams. 4 offensive players play hot potato (blocks and elbows), no dribbles allowed. Goal is to complete 20 passes — players count out loud. If defence tips a pass they get a point — their goal is 3 tipped passes. Ball in the air, feet in the air. Once goal is complete the ball is live for a single score or stop. 10-second timer.

2 Dribbles Pass
Passing Ball Handling
▶
4 players (wings and corners, matching bibs in pairs) pass between each other. Wings take 2 dribbles to elbow (must touch paint) and kick out to opposite corner. Corner releases to near-side wing, repeat. Follow your pass to fill spots. Goal is 10 paint touches in 30 seconds. After goal or time runs out it turns into live 2v2. 10-second timer. Make it take it.

Dribble Race 1v2
Passing Ball Handling Defence
▶
Split into 4 teams. Line up in the corners with 1 ball per line. Players dribble around a defender at the elbow and sprint to a defender between the 3pt line and halfcourt. Must dribble around and back to elbow defender and fire a pass to the original corner. Options: 1-handed pass off dribble, jumpstop/fake/overhead pass, or weak hand only. No chest passes. Zip the pass — elbow and wrist lock out as you step towards target. Defenders can't slide but can steal.
Scoring
Steal = 10 pts · Completed pass = 5 pts · Target: 50 pts. O/D team scoring.

Basic Shell
Defence System
▶
Start 3v3, space along wing and deep corner. Pass around offensive players and defenders move on a string. Build up to 5v5. Someone must cover Home and the Nail. Cover 2 guy is never the Home guy. Teach effective steps in and out of key to avoid 3-second violations.

Man in the Hole
Defence
▶
Defender is full-court pressing 1 ball handler. Shrink court to thirds and force ball handler to zig-zag. For every change of direction the ball handler makes, the defender gets a point. Guys on the side call out points. Defender plays 3 consecutive possessions up and down the floor. New ball handler every time. Defender must slide, or turn hips and sprint to recover.

1v1 Closeout
Defence Shooting
▶
O and D teams. Coach at Nail. Players paired up. Player A in deep corner (or wing), Player B on weakside block. Player B passes to coach and sprints to close out the corner. Coach passes to Player A for a shot or drive. Defender closes out chest first, legs wide and choppy feet. Arm up on the shot — don't leave feet. If they drive, force baseline and use a low active hand (reach upwards, not downwards to avoid fouls). Offense gets corner and wing before teams swap.
Scoring
Paint make = 10 pts · 3pt make = 5 pts · Midrange = 1 pt · Steal = 15 pts · Block = 10 pts. O/D team scoring.

1v1 No Arms
Defence
▶
Defender starts on the nail (or block). Offensive player starts at either top, wing or corner. Defensive player plays 3 consecutive possessions. Must hold a basketball (or medicine ball) above head the entire time. Aim to slide and keep offensive player out of paint. Every shot outside paint that isn't a 3 is a point for the defender. First to 3 points wins.

Dusty 1v1
Defence Finishing
▶
Split into groups of 4. 1 defender at FTL, 2 bystanders at elbows, 1 offensive player with ball at top. Defender taps either elbow player to trigger offense to attack. 1 dribble allowed — can pass to either elbow to reset. Make it take it, rotate: defence to elbow, elbow to offence.
Scoring
Paint finish = 5 pts · 3pt shot = 3 pts · Stop = 5 pts · Target: 15 pts. Individual scoring.

Defence Shooting
▶

Defence Finishing
▶
Players in pairs start with touch passes until offensive player changes direction and speed to trigger live 1v1. No dribbles allowed — will travel. Emphasise creativity of movement and focus on finishing. 30 seconds, stay on O/D. Highest score wins.

Ball Handling Defence

Can make it harder with a tennis or rugby ball. At halfcourt triggers live 1v1 going back. Multiple pairs at once to create chaos.

Paired Resistance
Ball Handling
▶
Players pair up and line up facing each other on the baseline. Player A (behind baseline) starts with ball. Player B places both hands on Player A's shoulders and pushes against them. Player A dribbles on the spot, using body to push against Player B. After 5 dribbles Player B disengages for Player A to sprint-dribble to halfcourt, jumpstop, pivot and back. Swap after each rep. 2 minutes. Best ball handlers use 2 balls.

Rip (Time Trial)
Ball Handling Finishing
▶
Single player time trial — hustle and footwork to never give up 50/50 balls. Coach lines up 6 balls on the floor (start around key, then out to corner, then out to wing). Player chooses any order to go and pick them up and attack the basket. Don't care about makes — care about footwork (no travels) and hustle. Eliminate wasted movement. Never stop the time trial. Coach and correct player afterwards. Film and show player straight away if possible.

2v1 Situationals
System Finishing
▶
Separate group into Hawks & Foxes, Cobras & Bears, Badgers. On one half: Option A (Pinch Post) with Hawks & Foxes. Hawk brings ball up from fullcourt run-up, Fox finds the elbow for a pin and seal. Other half: Option B (Hammer) with Cobras & Bears. Cobra starts at halfcourt, passes to coach at elbow, then plays tag/chase with their defender. Catches the eyes of Bear who sets a solid screen. Cobra sprints to corner for 3. Badgers/dev group run individual skills before joining a 2v1. Progress to 2v2, or 3v2 as weeks go on.

Creighton Transition
System Defence
▶
5v5. Offense on baseline, Defense along FTLE facing offense with numbers (1, 2 or 3). 1 is at the nail, 2s at elbows, 3s FTLE nearly at sideline. Coach calls a number — those players must touch the baseline before getting back on defence. Coach passes to any offensive player who attacks. Offense has 12 seconds to score. Defence must force ball to a side third and contain it. Late defenders recover to weakside. Defence: stop ball, cover Home, find the Nail (to close to a wing). Force a skip pass to the corner so we can trap.

Whistle Drill
System Defence
▶
5v5 half court. Teams space and run offensive pattern (not trying to score, just work the defence — if gap opens take it and kick out). Coach blows whistle and whoever has the ball must place it on the floor. Teams swap offense and defence and change matchups — the player guarding the ball cannot pick the ball up and continue.

Cone Fastbreak Spacing
System Finishing
▶
Split into teams of 5. Setup cones in 5 Green Eggs spots. Coach throws ball off backboard for a live ball rebound/inbounds. Players sprint to their spots — coach times and quickest into all 5 spots wins. Progress to add quickest into quick action score (Pinch Post layup or Hammer screen).

Get Action Corner to Wing
System Shooting
▶
O and D teams — defence given explicit instruction beforehand (ask players to discuss tactic). Offense in corner, coach at FTLE. Defenders execute tactic. Offense passes to coach for a Get action and looks for a 3. Read the defender — if needed flare off, pump fake and sidestep. Floor is lava — no inside 3 catches or dribbles. Offense has 1 shot each. Talk to each other to figure out coverage. Then swap and total score wins.
Scoring
Score each possession 1 point for O or D. Swap and total wins.

Home
System Shooting
▶
Drill the safety valve and corner shooter sprinting Home after every shot to eliminate fast break layups. Player starts on wing and top. Coach starts at weakside elbow. Wing swings to top. Top entry pass to elbow. Wing sprints to corner, top drifts to FTLE. Coach pass to corner for 3. On release both players race to Home. 1st player to Home wins a point.

Piano Warmup
Warmup Ball Handling
▶
Dribble ball on finger pads only. Use one finger at a time, work down then up fingers one by one. Ball should bounce extremely low to develop touch. 30 seconds per hand. Machine gun finish with both hands. Should take no longer than 2 minutes.

Pool Noodle Tag
Warmup
▶
Players line up around center circle and given a number — 2 players share each number (1s, 2s, 3s…7s). Coach holds 2 pool noodles. Players stutter-step, slide or step out-then-in around the circle. Coach calls a number — those 2 sprint to catch a noodle while everyone else escapes. Hitting someone tags them out. Escaping = getting behind a baseline. Sideline is not out. Go until a winning pair.

Warmup
▶
Split into Team A and B. Teams line up along baseline with 6ft between each person. Sprint between teammates in a slalom from left corner. When player gets to right corner they sprint to the middle to catch a slammed ball from coach. Each time ball bounces before player catches = 1 point. Fewest points wins. Teammates constantly clapping.

Warmup
▶
Players pair up with 1 ball. Racing sideline to sideline whilst holding ball between each other's backs. Not allowed to touch ball with hands or arms. Rhythm count for steps helps. Equal pressure into each other keeps ball in position. Ball drops = restart at nearest sideline. First to 3 successful trips wins.

▶

Warwickshire Hawks U17 · V10 Session Blueprint

📋 Session Structure

The standard session template. Follow this order and use the drill library to select and constrain within each block.

Global rules: All drills are make it take it unless stated. Presume 3 dribble max. Wherever possible, make drills competitive against live defence. The gym must be loud. High five or spud after every rep. Back-pedal/plyo if players are not on time or on target.
1
Warmup
Fun, engaging — build chemistry and touch
▶

Select from the Warmup category. Prioritise activities that build laughter and chemistry as well as touch. The energy level here sets the tone for the whole session. Involve the whole group, keep it moving.

Tip: Basket-Football, Pool Noodle Tag, and 5v5 w/ Yoga Ball are high-energy crowd-pleasers to open.
2
Stretching into Plyometrics
Stretching is always dynamic — plyometrics build bounce
▶

Dynamic stretching only — no static holds during warmup. Transition into plyometric work: bounds, box jumps, lateral explosive movements. The goal is to prime explosiveness, not reduce it.

Tip: Piano Warmup integrates well at the end of this block as a ball-touch bridge into the skills block.
3
Skills Drills Block
Always intro, then 1–2 detailed drills. Finishing OR Shooting
▶

If Finishing — use 3 Man Lays as intro.
If Shooting — use KD Form Shooting as intro.

Then mix in either Passing, Defence, or Ball Handling as the secondary skill block.

Structure: Intro drill (10 min) → Skill drill 1 (15 min) → Skill drill 2 (15 min) → Drink break.
4
Drink Break
Short break before Team Concepts
▶

Keep it brief. Use this moment to set up the Team Concepts block — brief the defence on their explicit coverage instructions (for Get Action, Creighton etc.) so they can discuss before drills begin.

5
Team Concepts
Always intro, then 2v1 detailed drills
▶

Hawks & Foxes run Option A (Pinch Post).
Cobras & Bears run Option B (Hammer).
Dev group runs individual workouts before joining others.

Progression: 2v1 → 2v2 → 3v2 as weeks go on.
6
Drink Break
Brief break before Games block
▶

Use this moment to set constraints for the Games block. Decide which system concepts to focus on and what scoring constraints will guide play.

7
Games
Use team concepts to guide and constrain play
▶

Always start with 2v2 or 3v3 as an intro before moving to 5v5. Use scoring constraints and explicit defensive instructions to reinforce the day's system concepts.

Teams who use ICE/WEAK ball screens, recover, and/or WOLF stay on.
8
Cooldown
Bring it together, open the floor
▶

Bring the group together. Ask for highlights and lessons learned. Open the floor for questions to clarify expectations, roles, and upcoming games. This is the culture moment — make it count.
