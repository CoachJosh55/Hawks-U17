System Archetypes

🦅 The Eagles

Ball Handler
▶
Offensive Trigger
  • Scan Before the CatchRead the floor before receiving so you can act immediately.
  • Pass Up the Floor EarlyPush pace before the defence sets — find the transition advantage.
  • Execute Option AInitiate the Pinch Layup to attack the paint on time.
  • Make the Open 3 on the Kick-OutWhen the drive collapses help, find and hit the open shooter.
Defensive Identity
  • On-Ball PestDisrupt the opponent's rhythm — make every dribble and catch uncomfortable.
  • Force the DirectionDictate where the ball handler goes; never let them choose freely.
0.5s Rule Scan & Move

🐍 The Cobras

Wing Shooter
▶
Offensive Trigger
  • Hammer CutAttack the corner off the cut for Option B (Corner 3).
  • Swing to Option ERelocate to the wing for the open 3 — elite feet-set speed required.
  • Relocate RelentlesslyNever stand still; always present a threat on the perimeter.
Defensive Identity
  • Transition Safety ValveBe the last line in transition — do not gamble for steals at the wrong moments.
  • Release = Sprint to the PaintThe moment the opponent releases, sprint to protect the paint.
Core Rule Relocate or Strike

🦊 The Foxes

Mid-Post Connector
▶
Offensive Trigger
  • Seal the Elbow / Pinch PostEstablish position to be a hub — receive, read, and distribute.
  • Hit the Cutter (Option A)Deliver on time, on target to the cutting Hawk.
  • Face Up & Attack (Option C)When the defence concedes the catch, attack the basket off the face-up.
Defensive Identity
  • Force ICE or Weak SideTake away the ball handler's preferred direction — channel into the help.
  • High-IQ Help RotationsRead the play two steps ahead and rotate before the problem arrives.
Fox Motto Find the Better Look

🐻 The Bears

Interior Anchor
▶
Offensive Trigger
  • Set Stationary ScreensLock in and set clean screens to free the Cobra for Option B.
  • Open to Ball & Roll (Option D)Pin-screen, open to the ball, and roll hard to the rim to receive.
Defensive Identity
  • Wall Up in the CylinderProtect the paint without fouling — be immovable, not reckless.
  • Cover Home in the Half-CourtHold your defensive anchor position and do not vacate the paint unnecessarily.
