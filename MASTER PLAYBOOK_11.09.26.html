Offence Overview

Offence Principles

Sequences of actions to cause chaos and force mistakes. We want layups and open 3s.

Consecutive Actions / Concepts
1

Pinch Post (Triangle Concept)

Three players form a triangle around the elbow/high post, strong-side wing & weakside wing. Using an entry pass, cuts and hand-off/ screen to create downhill baseline drives.

2

Hammer (Spurs 2014)

A baseline drive draws help before a weak-side player sets a back screen, freeing a corner shooter for a kick-out three behind the defence.

3

77 (Luka Dončić)

A double step-up screen action where the ball-handler attacks using two on-ball screens, creating opportunities to turn the corner, reject, hit the roller, or find shooters.

Alternate Actions to Deal with Breakdowns
4

Spain PnR (James Harden)

A ball-screen with a third player setting a back screen on the screener’s defender, creating confusion and opening the roll, lob, drive, or kick-out.

5

Loop (Tony Parker)

A guard loops around 3 baseline screens in quick succession & receiving the ball on the move toward the middle, creating an advantage for a pull-up, drive, or pass.

· Gravity = using your movement (cut/ drive/ flare/ hold) to occupy the attention of the defence

Base Offense Explained

GREEN EGGS

Pinch post -> Hammer -> 77

1) Setup

Eagles & Fox determine the strong side by setting up on an elbow and NBA wing (nearly our halfcourt).

2) Triangle

A safety Cobra is positioned on the weakside wing as a release valve (if the entry pass is denied). - TRIANGLE

3) Pinch Post & Hammer Action

Eagle executes entry pass to Fox and cuts towards basket looking for a layup. - PINCH POST

3A) When Fox receives ball Bear/ Badger sets a back screen for Striking Cobra - HAMMER

3B) Striking Cobra takes screen and cuts to weakside corner looking for a 3 - HAMMER

3C) After setting the screen the Bear/ Badger will roll to the basket - HAMMER

3D) Striking Cobra holds the corner regardless of whether they receive the ball - GRAVITY

4) Face Up & Drives

Fox Faces up as Eagle cuts to open sight lines for pass back to Eagle/ Corner 3/ Rolling screener/ Safety Cobra - PINCH POST

4A) If no passing lanes are open Fox is clear to drive/ shoot looking to score - GRAVITY

5) Flare

After cutting through the Eagles flares to same side corner and holds - GRAVITY

6) Ball Reversal

If no options yield a good shot the ball is reversed to the Safety Cobra on the wing - 77

7) 77 Action

The Fox & Bear/ Badger set a double step-up ball screen allowing the Cobra to attack the middle - 77

7A) 1 screener must roll - GRAVITY

7B) 1 screener must flare - GRAVITY

Execution Details

Player Tips

Player tips - Initial setup:

Eagle (Ball Handler)
✓ - Wait for the Fox's feet to be fully set at the elbow before passing. Timing is everything.
✗ - Don't pass while the Fox is still moving — a moving target fumbles the catch and kills the possession.
Fox (Pinch Post)
✓ - Flash to the elbow with purpose. Catch defenders in a lull. Present a firm target with both hands.
✗ - Don't drift past the elbow to the three-point line. The whole play depends on your position threatening the paint.
Badger/ Bear (Hammer Screener)
✓ - Stay patient. Your action starts when the ball hits the Fox. Use this time to read your defender.
✗ - Don't creep toward the Cobra early. The defence will see it and cheat the screen before it's even set.
Cobra (Shooter)
✓ - Stand still. Look bored. The less you move now, the more devastating the cut will be.
✗ - Don't drift toward the corner early. You'll drag your defender with you and telegraph the whole play.
Cobra (Safety)
✓ - Hold the weakside wing and read the whole floor. You are the reset button when things breaks down.
✗ - Don't drift toward the strong side. If the Eagle needs a bailout, you have to be exactly where expected.

Player tips - Pinch Post:

Eagle (Ball Handler)
✓ - Cut hard regardless. Sell it every time. Your commitment is what makes the fake dangerous.
✗ - Don't slow down to see if the handoff is coming. Decelerating lets your defender recover and closes both options at once.
Fox (Pinch Post)
✓ - Face up so you can read the space. Pass to an open cutter/ shooter, or punish your defender for helping.
✗ - Never attack baseline, unless you score a layup. Attacking middle opens the floor up for you, baseline closes the floor.
Badger/ Bear (Hammer Screener)
✓ - The moment the ball hits the Fox's hands is your cue. Timing starts from the catch, not the pivot.
✗ - Don't wait to see real or fake handoff — your job is identical either way. Move when the ball is entered.
Cobra (Shooter)
✓ - One hard step toward the basket to freeze your defender, then wait for the screen to arrive before cutting.
✗ - Don't cut before the screen is set. Running into an unset screen is an offensive foul every time.
Cobra (Safety)
✓ - Stay alert. If the Fox attacks off the fake, the play resolves quickly and transition defence starts.
✗ - Don't ball-watch and forget your defensive duty. If the Fox scores, someone needs to be the first back.

Player tips - Hammer:

Eagle (Ball Handler)
✓ - Look at the roller first. Eyes to the roller keep the Cobra's defender from cheating.
✗ - Don't look at the corner first. That telegraphs the kick pass and lets weakside defenders pre-rotate.
Fox (Gravity)
✓ - Hold the elbow, if your defender stops the Eagle you are the easiest cut & pass for a layup.
✗ - Don't clog the passing lanes by cutting early, we want the ball to reach the corner for a 3.
Badger/ Bear (Hammer Screener / Roller)
✓ - Set with your back towards the corner. Hold the screen for a full 2 second count, then roll with both hands up.
✗ - Don't roll before the screen is fully set. A roll-before-screen is called illegal every single time in a game.
Cobra (Shooter)
✓ - Run your defender into the screen. Brush shoulder-to-shoulder. The tighter you use it, the harder it is to fight over.
✗ - Don't drift around the screen. A wide cut gives the defender room to recover and makes the Hammer pointless.
Cobra (Safety)
✓ - If all options are covered you are the escape valve. Call a name or clap to help others find you.
✗ - Don't drift into the action. Your value is precisely that you're not in it — you hold the defence honest by staying put.

Player tips - 77:

Eagle (Gravity)
✓ - After your Pinch Post cut flare out to the same corner and hold. Your defender will be forced into a weak help position.
✗ - Don't drift up the court or clog inside the 3pt line. When the Cobra comes off the 77 you have to be ready to shoot.
Fox (77 Screener)
✓ - As soon as the ball reaches the safety Cobra that's your cue to set a step-up screen on the ball.
✗ - Don't roll/ flare too early. Hold the screen for a 2-second count and then attack hard.
Cobra (Gravity)
✓ - After your Hammer cut hold the corner to force your defender into a weak help position.
✗ - Don't drift up the 3pt line or inside the 3pt line. You'll clog the lanes for the rollers/ passers.
Cobra (Ball Handler)
✓ - If you can get a layup do it. If no one fights through the screens then take the 3pt shot. If the defence collapses find a shooter/ roller.
✗ - Don't leave any gaps for defenders to fight over the screens. Brush the screeners and read the other defenders.
Additional Sets

Additional Offense Explained

Spain PnR

Also referred to as Stack.

1) Both Cobras plant themselves deep in the corners, Fox & Badger/ Bear on each elbow

2) Eagle sets up in the middle between the 3pt line and half-court

3) Both the Fox and the Badger/ Bear set a step-up screen

3A) The Fox is setting a screen looking to roll

3B) The Badger/ Bear is setting a screen looking to flare

4) Eagle used the screens and attacks

5) Someone will be open, find the open man.

Loop

Also referred to as Tony.

1) Cobra (Screener) in weakside corner, Cobra (Zipper) under the basket, Fox/ Badger/ Bear on each elbow

1A) Eagle is on a wing - their choice, determines the strong side.

2) Fox & Badger/ Bear step into the paint and set an elevator screen.

3) Cobra (under the basket) zipper cuts up through the elevator screens looking to catch at the top.

4) Weakside Cobra, Fox & Badger/ Bear set staggered screens along the baseline for the Eagle to brush cut off

4A) Cobra flares to opposite corner

4B) Fox looks to seal their defender in the dunker spot

4C) Badger/ Bear looks to set a hammer screen on the Cobra at the top

5) Eagle catches on opposite side looking to attack middle

6) Someone will be open, find the open man.

SLOB

Green Eggs but setup with the Eagle as the inbounder. Everything else is exactly the same.

Defensive Architecture

Defensive System

No-Middle Man-to-Man Defense

One rule. Every player. Every possession. We take away the middle of the floor.

🛡️ The Core Rule: "Take away the Middle Third. Force baseline. Rotate together."

No-middle means every on-ball defender angles their body to funnel the ball-handler toward the Side Thirds. Never the Middle Third. Our help side is pre-loaded weakside.

1) On-Ball — Force Baseline (Ice / Weak)

Lead foot on the ball-handler shades them away from middle third. Guide them to the Side Thirds. Big calls "Ice" or "Weak" — Guard complies.

✗ NEVER allow a middle drive. Baseline is recoverable. Middle Third is a layup.

2) Help Side — Pre-loaded Weakside

First and second help defenders positioned one pass away on the Weak Side. Take away passing lanes, hands up in line with their man and the ball.

✗ Never stay silent, tell your teammates where you are and what's near them.

3) Baseline Drive — Full Rotation

When the ball beats their defender baseline: nearest weakside big cuts off the drive. On-ball defender chases from behind. All others rotate together — no one stands still.

✗ NEVER leave the strongside wing/ corner to rotate onto the ball. 1 pass away means it's an easy bailout.

4) Ball Screen — Ice / Weak / Drop / Under

Big calls "Ice" to force baseline — Guard complies, staying angled. By default big is in "Drop". Big shadows the ball within arm's reach of screener, protecting the rim. By default guard goes "Under". Guard goes beneath the screen to stop drives.

✗ NEVER switch ball screens by default. Switches create middle-driving mismatches.

5) Clipped / Reject — Communicate

"Clipped" — ball defender hit by a screen, handler turns the corner. Call it immediately.

"Reject" — ball handler avoids the screen to attack the basket. This must not be allowed — it beats our Ice coverage.

✗ NEVER go silent when you're clipped. Help defenders need the call to rotate.

6) Wolf — On-Ball Trap

Aggressive Trap after an Iced or Weaked ball screen - in the side thirds (so sideline is an extra defender). Cover tightly with an arm up to deter skip passes to the other side. Stunt & Recover if needed — buy time for teammates to close out.

✗ NEVER gamble for the steal when trapping. Stay disciplined and force a bad decision.

7) Home — Get Back First

On any transition, the deepest player must get "Home" — head under the basket. The Home position is the anchor. Cover 2 starts here: one defender, two offensive players (corner cutter + Crackback).

✗ NEVER gamble on a steal when you're supposed to be getting Home.

8) Top Lock — Elite Shooters

Facing a great shooter: angle your body to the high side to prevent catches above the 3pt line. Force baseline only. Never let them catch above the arc and get a free look. Backdoor/ burn cuts are inevitable, must rely on teammates to rotate.

✗ NEVER go under a screen when Top Locking — fight over to deny the catch above the line.

9) Communication — Always Talking

Call screens ("Screen left!"), drives ("Help!"), and rotations ("I've got baseline!", "Corner!") on every single possession. Silent defence is broken defence — even when you're perfectly positioned.

✗ NEVER go silent. Talking costs nothing. Getting scored on does.
